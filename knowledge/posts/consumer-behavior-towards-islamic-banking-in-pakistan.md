| **ABSTRACT/SUMMARY:** | Islamic banking has gained increasing popularity in Pakistan, with the number of Islamic banks and Islamic banking branches growing steadily over the years. However, there is a lack of research on consumer behavior towards Islamic banking in the country. This study aims to investigate the factors affecting customer adoption and usage of Islamic banking in Pakistan. The research is based on a survey of 500 customers of Islamic banks in major cities of Pakistan. The findings of this study can help Islamic banks in Pakistan to understand the consumer behavior towards their products and services, and to develop effective marketing strategies to attract and retain customers. | Islamic banking has gained increasing popularity in Pakistan, with the number of Islamic banks and Islamic banking branches growing steadily over the years.
