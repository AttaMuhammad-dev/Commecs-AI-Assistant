Smile — if you return from loss or defeat,  
Maybe someone else needed that win more, indeed.

Smile — if something has slipped away,  
It found the one destined to have it someday.

Smile — even if your heart feels torn,  
To heal one soul, another must mourn.

And if pain still whispers within your chest,  
Share it, and smile — you’ll find your rest.

And if your heart holds too much delight,  
Repeat the same process — smile bright.
