| **ABSTRACT/SUMMARY:** | In Pakistan, the performance of banking sector is influenced by deregulation, financial modernization and technological improvement. As financial sector is the back bone of the sustainable economic growth, so it is very important to assess the negative shocks in order to maintain the financial stability in Pakistan. This study investigates the impact of bank specific, industry specific and macroeconomic variables on the profitability of banks. For this purpose, top 10 listed scheduled banks of Pakistan are selected on the basis of availability of information. The empirical data of these banks are collected for the period from 2010 to 2018. The findings of this study can help banks, government, investors, policymakers, and shareholders for decision making and improving the performance of financial institutions in the future. | In Pakistan, the performance of banking sector is influenced by deregulation, financial modernization and technological improvement.
