## Mr. Inam Rahmani

Mr. Inam Rahmani is the Vice Chairmen of Commecs Educational Trust – CET.

Inam Rahmani is actively involved in various domains, showcasing a versatile career. He serves as a trustee for the Commecs Educational Trust and is recognized for pioneering Exhibition and Event Logistics in Pakistan. Beginning with the coordination of logistics for “IDEAS 2000, International Defense Exhibition” he remained involved in such ventures until his retirement in 2014, significantly elevating Pakistan’s presence in the Exhibition World. Inam Rahmani also held prominent roles, including Chairman and major shareholder, at Agility F&E Logistics Pakistan Ltd.

His commitment to professional standards is evident through his active membership in IELA, First Pakistani to have been given membership of IELA a prestigious global trade association focusing on exhibition logistics. The organization’s strict entry requirements focusing on exhibition & event expertise assure the highest level of quality service. Its members are recognized as the industry leaders and niche providers in their own communities or regions as well as around the globe.

During his academic tenure at the Government College of Commerce and Economics, Karachi                         Inam Rahmani excelled in debating and elocution competitions, showcasing his communication skills. Additionally, he has contributed to community affairs, serving twice on the managing committee of Commecs Old Students Association (COSA).

Inam Rahmani being an Ace Golfer having two aces to his credit has been member of the Club Executive Committee for two consecutive terms. Overall, Rahmani’s varied experiences underscore his leadership, entrepreneurial drive, and dedication to diverse fields.
