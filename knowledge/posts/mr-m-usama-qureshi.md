## Mr. M. Usama Qureshi

With a rich professional journey spanning over two decades, Usama Qureshi stands as a paragon of leadership and innovative management. His career is marked by notable success in diverse sectors such as Energy, FMCGs, Telecom, FinTech, and Industrial Production.

Usama’s distinguished career is highlighted by his current leadership as Vice Chairman of Cnergyico Pk Limited, where he spearheads strategic initiatives to drive sustainable growth. As Chief Executive at Oasis Energy Private Limited, he champions the development of innovative energy solutions. His advisory role at Kuickpay reflects his commitment to advancing Pakistan’s FinTech sector.

His transformative impact was previously showcased during his tenure as CEO & Managing Director at Hamdard Laboratories Pakistan, where he led the turnaround of a century old organization. At K-Electric Limited, as Chief of Staff and Chief Marketing Officer, Usama was pivotal in business transformation efforts, setting new benchmarks for efficiency and productivity. A notable highlight of Usama’s career was his pioneering work at Pakistan State Oil, where he revitalized the fuel cards business, introducing innovative practices that significantly enhanced the oil industry’s service standards.

As a fervent advocate for economic development, Usama holds the esteemed position of Senior Vice Chairman at the Pak-UK Business Council of FPCCI, working tirelessly to promote Pakistani businesses on a global scale. His previous roles in honorary capacity within FPCCI, the Pakistan Advertisers Society (PAS), and other industry associations underscore his dedication to fostering a vibrant business community in Pakistan.
