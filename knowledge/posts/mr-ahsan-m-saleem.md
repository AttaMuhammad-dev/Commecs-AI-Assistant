## Mr. Ahsan M. Saleem

Mr. Ahsan M. Saleem, is the Chairman of the Board of Governors, Commecs Institute of Business and Emerging Sciences, a degree awarding Institute for Business Administration program which is sponsored by the Commecs Educational Trust (a not-for-profit organization). He is the former Managing Trustee of the Commecs Educational Trust and former Chairman of the Managing Committee of Commecs College.

Professionally, he is the Group C.E.O. of Crescent Steel and Allied Mills Ltd. A graduate of the Govt. College of Commerce and Economics, he finished his further studies from Harvard University, U.S.A.

A renowned philanthropist in his own right, he is one of the founder members and the former Chairman of TCF – The Citizens Foundation, which has to its credit over 900 schools, all over Pakistan, established for the teaching of under privileged students of low income areas.
