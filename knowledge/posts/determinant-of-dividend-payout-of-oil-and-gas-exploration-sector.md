| **ABSTRACT/SUMMARY:** | This study aims to investigate the determinants of dividend payout of Oil and Gas Sector of Pakistan using secondary data from published annual reports from 2010 to 2019 listed on PSE (Pakistan Stock Exchange). Dividend payout can be affected by debt to equity ratio. Current ratio, profitability and corporate tax. Panel data technique used due to panel characteristics of available data with ordinary least square regression model to find out the impact of set of explanatory variables on the dividend payout using the Stata. Current ratio. Profitability and corporate tax have positive impact on dividend payout ratio whereas debt to equity ratio has negative impact on dividend payout ratio. | This study aims to investigate the determinants of dividend payout of Oil and Gas Sector of Pakistan using secondary data from published annual reports from 2010 to 2019 listed on PSE (Pakistan Stock Exchange).
