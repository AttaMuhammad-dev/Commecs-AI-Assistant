| **ABSTRACT/SUMMARY:** | This study compares the performance of the KMI-30 Index and the KSE-30 Composite Index of the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) during the period January 2010 to December 2016. Both the raw and risk-adjusted returns were calculated for the indices for the whole and two sub-periods. Results based on the raw returns revealed that generally, the KMI-30 and KSE-30 recorded the same level of returns. Tests using performance measures of Adjusted Sharpe Index, Treynor Index and Adjusted Jensen Alpha revealed that there was also no significant difference in the (risk-adjusted) performance of both indices. We therefore conclude that Sharia-approved stocks were not more favorable than the other stocks in the Pakistan stock exchange (PSX). | This study compares the performance of the KMI-30 Index and the KSE-30 Composite Index of the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) during the period January 2010 to December 2016.
