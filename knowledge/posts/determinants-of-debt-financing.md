| **ABSTRACT/SUMMARY:** | This paper helps us to understand and analyze the impact of different elements on debt financing of KSE-100 Index companies of Pakistan. Our main goal is to identify the frame works that enable us to understand the factors which influence the debt financing decision of companies. In this research we use debt financing as dependent variable while firm size, market capitalization, profit volatility & profitability as independent variables. 5 years of data 2014-2018 has been collected for 57 KSE-100 INDEX companies. For the collected data the method used in this research is Panel Least Square to find out the Impact of Independent Variables On Dependent Variables. The result shows positive impact of firm size on debt financing and negative impact of Marker capitalization & profitability on debt financing whereas there is no impact of profit volatility on debt financing due to insignificant results. | This paper helps us to understand and analyze the impact of different elements on debt financing of KSE-100 Index companies of Pakistan.
