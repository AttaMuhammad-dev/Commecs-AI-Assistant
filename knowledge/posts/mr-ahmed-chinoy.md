## Cdr (Retd) Tanveer Ahmad TI (M)

Management

Commander (R) Tanveer Ahmad, TI (M) took over as Principal Commecs College Jauhar Campus on August 7, 2019.

Cdr. (R) Tanveer possesses Master degrees in Physics, War Studies (Maritime) and Administrative Science from BZU Multan, NDU Islamabad and Karachi University, respectively. He was awarded with Tamgha-e-Imtiaz (Military) in 2006 for his meritorious services in Pakistan Navy.

Cdr. (R) Tanveer Ahmad possesses vast teaching and administrative experience having spent over 26 years in Pakistan Navy’s Education branch. He served in multi-disciplinary educational environments of the Navy starting his career with an institute imparting technical training to Pakistan Navy sailors and apprentices. He has the distinct honour of serving Pakistan Naval Academy twice in his career, where he taught and trained Cadets , the custodians of our beloved homeland. He had two stints each at Maritime Operations Training Complex and Pakistan Navy Engineering College – a constituent of NUST. He did his Staff Course from Pakistan Navy War College Lahore and had the honour to serve there as faculty member and Directing Staff (Plans). He also has the honour of serving higher headquarters to look after matters of not only Bahria Colleges, Bahria Schools in coastal areas of Balochistan, but also the Cadet Colleges under administrative control of Pakistan Navy. Towards the end of his career, he has been Director of Studies and Fleet Education & Training Officer.
