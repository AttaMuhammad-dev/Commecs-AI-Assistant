Commecs College-An Overview
The Foundation Stone of Commecs Institute of Business Education (CIBE) was laid in 1988. An extensive
fund-raising campaign was launched and land was purchased in Gulistan-e-Jauhar, Karachi. A purpose-
built three-storey building with a covered area of 1.24 acres was inaugurated by the then Sindh Governor,
Late Hakim Mohammed Said, on September 15, 1993. The campus facilities are dedicated in the name of
donors.
In 2003, Pre-engineering discipline was introduced necessitating a change in name from Commecs
Institute of Business Education (CIBE) to Commecs Institute of Business and Emerging Sciences (CIBES).
Subsequently Pre-Medical and Computer Science programs were also launched and CIBES were converted
into two institutions; The CIBES itself and the Commecs College. All Intermediate study programs came
under Commecs College. In persuit of more fruitful contribution to the society, Commecs College introduced
Humanities discipline in 2021.
Campus facilities include comfortable air-conditioned classrooms equipped with multimedia, a high-
tech Computer laboratory, Physics, Chemistry and Biology laboratories, a well-stocked library, a spacious
auditorium, training room, prayer hall, cafeteria, tuck shop, girls common room and indoor sports facilities.

## Prospectus PDF page 17

Uninterrupted Academic Session: In an environment where closure of institutions or breakdown of academic
sessions is an unfortunate reality, Commecs College maintains an uninterrupted academic session. A detailed yearly
calendar of academic, co/extra-curricular activities and events is given to students at the commencement of the
session. Activities are held in accordance with the calendar. Commecs College has arrangements for solar based
alternate power generation other than KE Supply.
Quality Assurance: Commecs College Management takes measures for maintaining quality education. Academic
audit of educational services is carried out by a third party. The process starts with class observations, resource
material, Examination assessments strategies, faculty development and students satisfaction. Teachers prepare
portfolios containing lesson plans with short and long term planning, record of question papers, assessments,
remedial measures, support material, personal evaluation and academic achievements to identify strengths and
weaknesses of students. The purpose of this exercise is to assure quality in academic process.
Student Parent Teacher Meetings (PTM): PTMs are conducted after
every internal examination, to inform parents about the student’s
academic progress, examination result, class participation,
discipline, attendance and co-curricular activities. Parents are
advised to attend these meetings in order to develop a rapport with
the institution for the student’s overall development as an individual
and a responsible citizen of the country.
