## Admission Criteria

*   Applicants are required to abide by the instructions given in the admission prospectus
*   It is the sole responsibility of the applicant to go through the contents of the prospectus in detail before applying at Commecs College
*   Provisional admission is given on the basis of merit determined through candidate’s result of SSC-Part I or its equivalent examinations, admission test and interview

##### The merit assessment comprises:

*   Previous Scholastic Achievement
*   Commecs Admission Test (CAT)
*   Interview

  
Admission at Commecs College is strictly merit based. Recommendation and/or use of influence to secure admission will result in disqualification of the candidate.
