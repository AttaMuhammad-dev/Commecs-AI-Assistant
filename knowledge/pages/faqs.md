## FAQs

1\. When does admission start in Intermediate?

Admission process normally begins in April and continues till July. New session starts from August 1st, each year. For further details, please visit College website.

2\. Is admission possible in your college against donation?

Admissions are not granted on the basis of donation.

3\. Is there any quota fixed for the disabled in Commecs College?

Quota is not fixed for the disabled. Admissions are offered purely on merit.

4\. Is financial assistance provided to students?

Yes, Commecs College offers merit and need based (Endowment) scholarships to students on a set criteria.

5\. Is Admission given on the basis of Sports?

No admission is granted on the basis of Sports.

6\. Is there any age relaxation possible?

Applicants must not be above 18 years of age on August 1st, in the year of application. Relaxation up to 1.5 years is given to Huffaz-e-Quran (candidates are required to show their “Sanad”)

7\. What is the policy for taking admission in Second Year?

Admissions are offered in First Year only.

8\. Is the College for boys or girls or co-educational?

Commecs College is a co-education institution.

9\. Does the college offer transport facility?

College arranges a contractor to facilitate students.

10\. Can we know about the environment of the College?

College offers safe and secure environment for students. Classrooms and facilities are air-conditioned. Discipline is the hallmark of Commecs College. Campus monitoring through CCTV ensures security of students.

11\. What sports facilities are available at Commecs College?

College has in-campus facilities for Table Tennis, Volley Ball, Basketball, Tennis and Throw Ball. College teams participate in Cricket, Squash, Basketball, Badminton, Volley Ball, Throw Ball, Football, Hockey and Tennis tournaments.

12\. Does college offer opportunities for academic and personal development?

There are a wide range of co-curricular activities which enhance a student’s personal development, written and spoken skills including in-house competitions like Bilingual Declamations and Essay Writing Competitions. Students also participate in inter-collegiate competitions.

There are number of clubs and societies. Students can join a club or society of their own choice.

13\. What are the opportunities for Commecs College students for exposure to industry and management processes in the business field?

Students’ Field Trips to different industries and organizations are a regular feature.

14\. Are there any guidance and Counselling services for students?

The college has a students’ counselor to work for mental well-being of students. Career Counselling Sessions are also held for students. College alumni network also facilitates in this aspect.
