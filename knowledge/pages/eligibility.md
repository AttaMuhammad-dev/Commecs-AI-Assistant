## Eligibility

**Note: Any misstatement of information will automatically cancel admission without any claim on the Commecs College**

##### Requirements

*   65% and above marks in SSC/equivalent examination for Pre-Engineering, Pre-Medical and Computer Sciences disciplines
*   60% and above marks in SSC/equivalent examination for Commerce discipline.
*   Candidates with Cambridge background are required to clear 8 papers with 4 compulsory papers (Urdu, English, Pakistan Studies and Islamiat) and one Mathematics paper from optional subjects
*   Candidates with SSC General Group are NOT eligible to apply for admission in Science Group (Pre-Engineering or Pre-Medical)
*   Candidates who have studied Computer Studies in lieu of Biology in SSC or its equivalent examination are NOT eligible to apply for admission in Pre-Medical Group
*   Candidates who have not studied Biology/Computer Science in O’ Level Examination are not eligible to apply for admission in Science (Pre-Engineering or Pre-Medical groups)

##### Age Limit

*   Applicants must not be above 18 years of age as on August 1st, in the year of application. However, those more than 18 years of age but less than 19 can be admitted subject to approval of the Chairman-CET.
*   Relaxation up to 1.5 years is given to Huffaz-e-Quran (candidates are required to show their “Sanad”)

##### On-line Registration

Applicants are required to register themselves online on our website. 

##### Documents to be submitted

*   Photocopy of marks sheet / transcript / promotion certificate obtained in SSC-I/O’ Level Examinations
*   Photocopy of Birth Certificate / B. Form
*   Photocopy of SSC-II Admit Card/Statement of Entry
*   Two passport size photographs
*   Admission Processing Fee of Rs. 1,000/=
*   Hifz Sanad (For Huffaz Only)
