## Requirement for First Year Admission 2026

#### Student's Name:

**Candidates are required to carefully fill the online registration form. They are to write their own and father’s name as given in their SSC/O Level Marks Sheet.**

#### Minimum Requirement for Applying:

*   Passed SSC-I/O Level with 65% results for Science Group.
*   Passed SSC-I/O Level with 60% results for Commerce Group.

**Direct Admission to Students with 85% or more marks in SSC/Equivalent Exams.**
