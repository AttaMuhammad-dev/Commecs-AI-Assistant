## Sibling Discount

##### **Sibling Discount****:**

*   25% discount in Tuition Fee to students whose brother (s) / sister (s) is/are studying in Commecs College in the same academic year.
*   25% discount in Tuition Fee is offered to 02 siblings if 03 or more are studying in the same academic year.
