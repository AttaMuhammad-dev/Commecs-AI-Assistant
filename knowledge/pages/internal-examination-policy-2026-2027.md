## INTERNAL EXAMINATION POLICY 2026–2027

#### 1\. Purpose

This policy establishes clear procedures for conducting examinations and assessments fairly, consistently, and in accordance with the academic standards of Commecs College.

#### 2\. Types of Examinations

*   **Formative Assessments:** Quizzes and Assignments
*   **Summative Assessments:** Mid-Term and Final Examinations

####   
3\. Examination Schedule

Examinations will be conducted according to the Academic Calendar. Any changes will be communicated in advance through official college channels, including the notice board, Parent & Student Whatsapp, or college website. 

#### 4\. Assessment & Weightage

| **Component** | **Weightage** |
| --- | --- |
| Mid-Term Examination |     **60%** |
| Quizzes & Assignments |     **40%** |
| **Total** |    **100%** |

Students must achieve a minimum **C Grade** in **all subjects** in the Internal Examinations.

#### 5\. Grading System

| **Percentage** | **Grade** | **Description** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **95–100%** | **A++** | Exceptional |
| **90–94%** | **A+** | Outstanding |
| **85–89%** | **A** | Excellent |
| **80–84%** | **B++** | Very Good |
| **75–79%** | **B+** | Good |
| **70–74%** | **B** | Fairly Good |
| **60–69%** | **C** | Above Average |
| **50–59%** | **D** | Average |
| **40–49%** | **E** | Below Average |
| **Below 40%** | **U** | Unsatisfactory / Fail |

#### 6\. Examination Conduct

*   Students must report **10 minutes before** the examination.
*   Students arriving more than **30 minutes late** will not be permitted to enter.
*   Students must bring their own stationery. Sharing is not permitted.
*   Cheating or use of unfair means will result in cancellation of the paper and disciplinary action.

#### 7\. Absentee Policy

*   An absent student will be marked **“A”** and treated as **Fail**, unless the absence is approved.
*   In case of illness or emergency, a valid medical certificate or parent/guardian note must be submitted within **2 working days**.

#### 8\. Use of Technology

Mobile phones, smart watches, and unauthorized electronic devices are strictly prohibited during examinations. Any device found may be confiscated and may result in disciplinary action.

#### 9\. Parental Involvement

Parents/guardians will be informed about examination schedules and student performance. Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTMs) may be conducted after examinations to discuss academic progress.

#### 10\. Cheating Policy: Zero Tolerance

Cheating is strictly prohibited. The College follows a zero-tolerance policy toward cheating in all examinations. Any student found cheating will face strict disciplinary action according to the College rules.

*   Marks deduction
*   Paper cancellation
*   Suspension or expulsion in serious or repeated cases

#### 11\. Policy Amendment

Commecs College reserves the right to amend or update this policy when necessary. Students and parents/guardians will be notified of significant changes through official communication channels.
