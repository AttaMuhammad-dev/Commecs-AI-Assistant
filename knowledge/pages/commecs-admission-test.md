## Commecs Admission Test

Applicants are required to take the Commecs Admission Test (CAT) irrespective of their previous system of education which provides a fair measure of a candidates’ scholastic attainment as well as an insight into their potential. Separate tests are designed for Science, Commerce, Science General (Computer Science) and Humanities candidates and are conducted to assess an applicant’s skills in the following areas:
